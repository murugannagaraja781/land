📌 How to Request Account Deletion (கணக்கை நீக்குவது எப்படி?)

In compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, users of Tenkasi Dreams Land can request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

  1. From Within the App: Open Tenkasi Dreams Land → Go to Account (சுயவிவரம்) → Tap Settings → Click Delete Account (கணக்கை நீக்கு).
  2. From this Webpage: Submit the simple verification form below or email us directly at admin@tenkasidreams.com with your registered phone number.

🗑️ What Data is Deleted? (என்னென்ன தரவுகள் நீக்கப்படும்?)

Upon receiving your deletion request and verifying ownership, we permanently remove the following records within 7 business days:

  • User Profile: Name, phone number, email address, and profile picture.
  • Property Listings: All advertisements, land/house photos, and property descriptions posted by your account.
  • Chat & Inquiries: Your chat messages, saved favorites, and search history.
Data Retention: Records of legal invoices or billing receipts (if any) may be retained for up to 90 days solely to comply with applicable taxation and statutory financial recordkeeping laws, after which they are permanently purged.
